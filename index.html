Secret Santa Rules

1. Each participant writes a wishlist for their Secret Santa.

2. It's usually your Original Characters or a favorite character from a show/videogame, but it can be anything, a portrait of yourself, your pet, loved ones, etc.

3. On November 25th, we will assign a Secret Santa to each participant. (be sure to have your wishlist ready by then)

4. Your task as a Secret Santa will be to draw a character from your assigned victim's wishlist. 😈

5. On December 25th, everyone will send their gift on the WhatsApp group chat.
